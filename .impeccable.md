## Design Context

### Users

myDAUST serves students and university staff. First-time students may be completing account activation in person during a busy school-year launch, without email access. Students also use the portal to understand annual services without mistaking a request for an approved change. Finance staff configure annual billing services and adjustments through approval-backed workflows, while Registrar and administrator staff operate annual housing assignments without changing Finance-owned prices or losing historical room evidence.

### Brand Personality

Trustworthy, calm, authoritative, and operational. The interface should feel like a modern university service: precise enough for identity and financial work, humane in its language, and composed under operational pressure.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the established DAUST visual system: navy and deep navy as the institutional foundation, orange only for focused action and emphasis, steel-tinted neutrals, Saira for display type, and Montserrat for body copy. Favor a refined, light-first, dense-but-readable layout with strong hierarchy and restrained motion. Preserve the existing token-based dark mode. Avoid generic card grids, gradient-heavy SaaS styling, decorative glass effects, or playful treatments that weaken trust.

### Design Principles

1. Optimize account activation for a three-minute, self-service check-in: one decision at a time, precise credential language, and recoverable expiry, mismatch, and reissue states.
2. Make authority visible: clearly distinguish current state, requested state, and approved state.
3. Preserve operational context: show academic year, price, billed option, capacity, and warnings beside the action they govern.
4. Fail clearly and accessibly: keep validation, stale-state, pending, success, and error feedback close to the affected control.
5. Meet WCAG AA expectations with keyboard access, visible focus, semantic status updates, reduced-motion respect, no color-only meaning, and consistent responsive behavior in light and dark themes.
